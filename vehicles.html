Before you enquire

A few useful answers.

Are the pictured vehicles available to buy?

The photographs illustrate the service. They are not stock listings. Ask about a specific model through the enquiry form; availability is confirmed individually.

Can I enquire without a model in mind?

Yes. Tell us the vehicle type, intended use, destination and any essential features. Your budget range is useful if you have one.

What about inspection, delivery or warranty?

Ask for the details that apply to the particular vehicle. Condition, inspection options, delivery, warranty and payment terms must be confirmed in writing before a purchase.

A clear business introduction

A focused web presence for a business that needs to explain what it does and help people make contact. Begin with the audience, core offer and the next action visitors should be able to take.

  • Business story and core service content
  • Responsive layouts and clear contact routes
  • Domain, content and handover responsibilities

A fuller company website

A broader site for multiple services, a growing team or regular updates. The scope should make content ownership and the publishing process as clear as the public-facing pages.

  • Service pages and company information
  • Content editing and publishing scope
  • Search foundations and measurement plan

A product catalogue or online store

Help visitors compare items through useful product information, categories and filters. If ordering or payment is needed, define the fulfilment, refund and administration processes before selecting the checkout flow.

  • Product data and category structure
  • Ordering and payment requirements
  • Stock updates, fulfilment and administration

A coherent brand toolkit

Turn a business direction into a usable identity across documents and customer touchpoints. The useful output is a consistent system with agreed file formats, usage guidance and ownership.

  • Logo and identity application scope
  • Document and social format requirements
  • Final files, usage guidance and rights

Content for a specific campaign

Build photography, graphics or video around the audience and the campaign’s intended action. Formats, approvals, usage rights and where the work will appear should be agreed before production.

  • Campaign objective and audience
  • Content formats and production responsibilities
  • Review rounds, usage rights and release plan

A simpler business workflow

Map the repeated job before adding software. Bookings, enquiries, reporting and customer updates work better when ownership, exceptions and the handover between people are understood.

  • Current process and intended improvement
  • Roles, permissions and information needs
  • Testing, training and ongoing support scope

    Before you decide.

    Are these fixed-price packages?

    No. They are package directions for scoping. Price and delivery dates depend on the agreed content, functionality, production work and support.

    Is THE 97 World partnership already executed?

    No executed partnership is being claimed. Collaboration and delivery responsibilities remain subject to confirmation.

    Who owns the finished website or brand files?

    Ownership, account access, third-party licences and handover formats should be stated in the written proposal before work is commissioned.

    Can a simple website grow into a catalogue or store?

    That can be considered in the scope. Start with accurate product information and define ordering, payment and fulfilment requirements before adding transactions.

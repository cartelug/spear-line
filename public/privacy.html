How information submitted through this website is used.

Website status: This website is in preparation. Online submission is not enabled. You can prepare and download an enquiry on your own device.

Who handles your enquiry

Spear Line uses the information you provide to discuss your vehicle or timber transport requirement.

What the form asks for

The form collects your name, email address or phone number, chosen service, service requirements and any additional details you choose to include. It also records your agreement to be contacted, a submission time and a reference number when online submission is enabled.

How the information is used

Enquiry details are used to understand your request and respond through the contact details you provide. Sending an enquiry does not create a booking, purchase agreement or obligation to proceed.

Preparing an enquiry on your device

When the form is in preparation mode, your entries stay in the page until you choose to download a text file. They are not submitted to Spear Line. Keep the downloaded file secure, especially if it contains personal contact details.

When online submission is enabled

Submitted enquiries are stored on the server for the person managing Spear Line enquiries to review. The form confirms receipt only after the server has stored the request. It does not take payments or upload documents.

Please avoid including identity documents, bank information or other sensitive personal details in the free-text field.

Website providers

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and representative photography from Unsplash. When those resources load, those providers receive technical information such as your IP address. Hosting providers may also process connection and security logs according to their configuration.

The website code does not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies or browser storage for enquiry details. A short-lived server rate limit helps prevent excessive form submissions; it is not stored with the enquiry.

Access, correction and deletion

Contact details for privacy requests will be published before online enquiries are enabled.

Enquiry records should be kept only as long as needed for the enquiry and any applicable record-keeping requirements. Before launch, the website operator must confirm the retention period and any additional local privacy requirements.

Photography and availability

The photographs illustrate the types of services discussed. They are not vehicle stock listings, evidence of owned vehicles, or photographs of completed Spear Line transport jobs. Availability, condition, routes and terms are confirmed for the individual enquiry.

Prepare a transport enquiry

The details drive the plan

A clear brief. A considered route.

Transport requirements differ from one load to the next. Share the timber form, estimated quantity, pickup access and destination so the scope can be discussed.

Timing, route feasibility, documentation responsibilities and price are confirmed for the specific movement before an agreement.

  • 01
    Pickup & destination

    An exact pickup location and delivery city or site. For DRC movements, include the specific destination.

  • 02
    Timber & load

    Describe the timber form and estimated volume or weight. Say if the quantity is still to be confirmed.

  • 03
    Preferred timing

    Your ready date, delivery window, and any loading or unloading constraints.

  • 04
    Supporting information

    Let us know the status of relevant documents and any site requirements. Share sensitive documents only through an agreed channel.

Before you enquire

Plan with the right information.

Is this a timber sales service?

This service is for timber transportation and logistics coordination. The website does not list timber inventory for sale.

Can you quote before I know the load size?

You can begin an enquiry with an estimate or mark the quantity as to be confirmed. A meaningful transport proposal needs the load, pickup location, destination and timing.

Are customs clearance and permits included?

Documentation, permits and border-related responsibilities depend on the movement. Confirm who is responsible for each requirement in the written scope before proceeding.

Is a delivery date guaranteed when I enquire?

An enquiry is the start of a discussion. Availability, route feasibility and delivery arrangements must be agreed for the individual movement.

How to get started

No guesswork. Just a clear brief.

Three steps from a first requirement to an enquiry you can send or keep.

  1. 01

    Choose a service

    Pick vehicle sales or timber transportation. You can switch before you finish.

  2. 02

    Describe your requirement

    Vehicle: model or type, destination and budget range. Timber: pickup, destination, estimated load and timing.

  3. 03

    Prepare your enquiry

    Prepare a downloadable draft of your enquiry. Online sending is not open yet, so nothing is submitted to Spear Line until it is.

Availability, price and arrangements are confirmed for each enquiry, in writing, before any commitment.

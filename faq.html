Getting started

What does Spear Line do?

Spear Line brings together vehicle sales and trading, foodstuffs and beverages, and logistics. Each starts with the same conversation: what do you need, where is it going and when?

What should I include in an enquiry?

Your name and how to reach you, then the details for your service. Vehicles: the model or type, a budget if you wish, and the destination. Supplies: products, quantities, delivery destination and timing. Logistics: pickup, destination, cargo and timing.

Is an enquiry a booking or a purchase?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Availability, specifications, price and arrangements are confirmed for each enquiry, and in writing, before you make any commitment.

Enquiries and privacy

Can I send an enquiry online?

Online submission is not currently open. You can prepare your enquiry on the website, which creates a downloadable draft. Nothing is sent to Spear Line until online submission opens. Check the contact page for direct channels.Yes. Choose a service on the enquiry page, complete the short form and send it. You will receive a reference once it is received.

What should I leave out of an enquiry?

Identity documents, card details, bank details and other sensitive information. You can add anything further once you are in conversation.

How is my information used?

To review and respond to your request. The privacy page explains what is collected, how long it is kept when online enquiries are enabled, and how to ask about your information.

About this website

Are the images real photographs?

No. The imagery on this website is AI-generated and illustrates our service areas. It does not show Spear Line staff, stock, facilities or vehicles, and is not a claim of completed work.

Do you list stock or prices here?

No. This website does not publish stock or price lists. Share what you need and the details are confirmed for your enquiry.

What is Spear Line Bar?

The next chapter of Spear Line. It is coming soon, and the location, opening details and menu will be announced on its page. Nothing is open or bookable yet.

Markup and competitors

Why 12% with a USD 500 minimum.

A flat 8% earns under USD 120 on a USD 1,500 car. That does not cover sourcing time, money transfer, exchange-rate movement or a deal that falls through. The owner sets the final markup.

Competitors

WhoWhat they offer
Exporters
BE FORWARD, SBI Motor, SAT Japan, EVERY, Tokyo Carz, JGLOGI		Sell the same cars to buyers directly at the plain FOB price. SBI delivers to Kampala and Nimule; SAT lists Juba.

Local sellers
Autochek Uganda, usedcars.co.ug, Kampala dealers; Light Heart General Trading and LTA-Juba		Cars already in the country. No Juba price lists found.

What it means for us

  • Buyers can go direct to exporters, so our fee has to be earned by delivery, clearing, registration and one written price.
  • No competitor publishes an all-in price for Kampala or Juba. A clear, correct landed price is how Spear Line can stand out.

Margin evidence

  • One Nairobi importer-dealer reports 10% to 15% before tax. A Kenyan blog's 15% to 30% is promotional.
  • No Uganda or South Sudan figure found. Local asking prices found were undated or secondary.

Next step. Ask two importers for written cleared-in-Kampala quotes on the three example cars, and collect Juba dealer prices, before the markup is final.

## 6. Art direction

Keep the V11 visual DNA instead of restarting needlessly: deep soil **#15110C**, dark umber **#211A13**, sand **#E9DFCE**, rich bone **#F5F0E7**, restrained golden highlight **#F2B65A** and a carefully limited teal accent. Gold directs attention; it should not flood every surface.

- Design language: elegant African corporate editorial, believable documentary photography, wide cinematic panels, oversized but readable type, intentional whitespace, disciplined image grids.
- Typography: existing licensed Brighter Sans Bold + Sky Text until licences and approved brand system are confirmed. Do not silently change fonts.
- Motion: polished entrances, restrained imagery parallax, editorial image masks, small nav transitions. Reduce motion must remove essential movement; content stays accessible without JS.
- Desktop: 12-column grid, max content width around 1360px, strong asymmetry and measured line length.
- Mobile: purposeful portrait crops, touch-safe buttons, simple full-screen navigation, short headings, no horizontal overflow or unreadable overlays.
- Global: preserve focus states, keyboard navigation, proper image alternative text, visible form errors, 44px minimum practical tap targets, and compressed AVIF/WebP derivatives.

## 7. Operations, performance, compliance and QA

- Existing V11 source deliberately has `enquiriesEnabled:false` and empty legal/contact settings. Do not imply forms are live. To launch, choose a monitored form receiver/backend, privacy retention, spam protection, verification and confirmation messages.
- Existing site is `noindex` without verified launch origin. Preserve until a real domain is approved.
- Keep the generator as source of truth (`content/site.mjs`, `scripts/build.mjs`) rather than hand-editing generated HTML.
- Release work must regenerate public and mirror outputs and pass `npm run build`, `npm run check`, `npm test` and Playwright E2E checks.
- Re-test all existing routes: index, vehicles, supplies, logistics, timber alias, how it works, about, bar, FAQ, enquiry, contact, privacy and 404.
- Set thresholds: target Lighthouse performance >=90 mobile, accessibility >=95 (aim 100), layout shift <0.1, clear no-hallucination content audit and real mobile Safari checks before release.
- Collect owner approvals: legal entity, division statuses, digital delivery relationship, founder biographies, image rights, company contacts, real portfolio and approval of any fee/scope expansion.

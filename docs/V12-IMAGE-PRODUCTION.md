# Spear Line V12 — image production and asset integration plan

**Status:** production plan, 7 Nov 2026. No new AI images have been represented as real company evidence. The photo references and asset list are subject to ownership and licensing checks.

## Visual goal

One connected visual world for a diversified African company: confident, warm, tactile and editorial. The camera perspective should feel like a premium commercial photojournalist rather than a collage or generic AI advertising banner. Prefer **real supplied photographs** for founder portraits, staff, vehicles, actual sites and completed business activities. Use generated images only for generic illustrative scenes that do not impersonate real company premises or transactions.

**Look:** natural golden-hour East African light; ochre/red earth, dark greens, warm concrete, weathered steel, soft amber highlights; finely controlled contrast; natural skin and cloth textures; believable product scale. Mix wide establishing views, 50mm environmental portraits, medium-range process photographs, details and quiet interior still life. Limit artificial lens flares and avoid hyperreal HDR.

**Negative rules for generated illustrations:** no illegible text, fake signage, logo lookalikes, invented license plates, trademarks, fantasy cargo geometry, extra fingers, melting steering wheels, duplicated humans, false uniforms, fake documents, simulated celebrity/founder likeness, fake completed projects, inflated fleet or invented offices. Avoid stock-photo handshakes and glowing tech holograms.

## Priority and production split

- **First choice from existing source:** Logo (unmodified), verified Spear Line operations photographs, approved founder headshots, true vehicle inventory and any authorised actual business site images.
- **Existing V11 illustrations to reuse temporarily:** road hero, SUV, pickup, loading bay, market produce, highland road, desk contact and backdrop derivatives. V11 already uses GPT-generated scenes; maintain the site disclosure while they remain.
- **Generated gap-filling batch 1:** images 01/02, 03/04, 11/12, 13/14, 17/18, 22/24, plus vectors 25/26. Keep generated visuals explicitly illustrative.
- **No synthetic headshot substitution:** 19/20/21 wait for approved photographs. The Drive contains three founder-labelled existing assets; check authenticity and permission before using.

## Composition and format specs

1. Desktop heroes: 2400×1350 master (16:9) or higher; preserve 35–45% text-safe space.
2. Phone heroes: 1080×1620 master (2:3); compose natively rather than centre-cropping a wide image.
3. Editorial feature images: 1800×1200 master (3:2) for prominent rows and 1200×1500 (4:5) for portraits.
4. Card images: 1200×900 (4:3), plus 800×1000 portrait variant where the interface uses vertical crops.
5. Founder portrait: at least 1600 px on its long dimension, uncompressed approved original retained offline.
6. Website delivery: AVIF responsive breakpoints 480, 768, 1080, 1536; WebP fallback; use accurate alt text and stable dimensions, lazy-load below the fold.
7. Small softened image backdrops: 480 px WebP with consistent contrast; generate from each source image.
8. File inventory: each asset gets source, owner/rights, date, page use, authenticity status (real/illustrative), output versions and final approval.

### Founder photography treatment (approved real photo only)

“Use supplied approved original photo as identity source. Make restrained editorial adjustments to exposure, background clutter and colour grade while preserving the subject’s actual face, skin appearance, clothing, hairstyle, body proportions and the truth of the setting. Do not insert the subject into a fictional business scene and present it as evidence. Export 4:5 portrait, landscape 3:2 variant if composition allows.”

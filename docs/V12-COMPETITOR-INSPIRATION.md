# Spear Line V12 — competitor research and original redesign decisions

Date: 7 November 2026
Status: Implemented on a draft GitHub branch, not merged or deployed to the public site.

## Three inspiration sources

1. [Simba Corporation](https://www.simbacorp.com/) — straightforward five-business navigation, strong editorial opening and sector-led destination pages. **Our response:** a six-division Spear Line grid with a distinct category, illustrative image, status and relevant next action per division.
2. [Madal General Company](https://madalmarket.com/) — trade-oriented categories, specific products/service journeys and visible quotation calls to action. **Our response:** keep vehicles, supplies and transport at the center of practical enquiry flows. Do not invent stock, prices, fuel depots, commercial contracts or statistics.
3. [AXIS GROUP South Sudan](https://www.axisgroupss.com/) — sector overview, clear technology capability cards, consultation pathways. **Our response:** a dedicated Digital Solutions page and a structured six-capability presentation with an honest partnership-status disclaimer.

## Implemented page architecture

- **Home:** expanded corporate positioning, overview of six divisions, vehicles/supplies/logistics entry points, digital feature, founder feature, business approach and coming-soon hospitality teaser.
- **Business Divisions:** six editorial business-area cards, stage labels, appropriate destinations, and cross-division logistics overview.
- **Digital Solutions:** websites, brand identity, systems/bookings, content/video, marketing and automation/infrastructure. THE 97 World relationship is explicitly proposed, pending approval.
- **Founder:** Joh Ayom, a monogram-led identity treatment while waiting for approved portraits, verified biography and a signed-off founder quote.
- **Navigation and footer:** business divisions, digital and founder links alongside legacy services. No removal of existing routes.
- **Responsive CSS:** new final-layer CSS file, with purpose-built typography, image overlays, bento cards, small-screen layouts and reduced-motion handling.
- **Existing routes:** Vehicles, Supplies, Logistics, How it Works, FAQ, About, Contact, Enquiry, Privacy, Bar (Coming Soon), and 404 remain supported.

## Originality and truth standards

Adapt **information architecture and interaction patterns**, not copyrighted website text, protected assets, logos, stock lists, metrics or proprietary software. The new Spear Line copy, page hierarchy, monogram and responsive styling are original to this project. All photos currently in use are the site's illustrative assets and are explicitly labelled as such. Do not imply that illustrations are Spear Line staff, real stock, property or facilities.

This is not a wholesale merger with competitors' offerings: only Spear Line's owner-approved business divisions and feasible services should be made public. The digital offering is a proposed partnership-enabled service, **not** a seventh company division or confirmed ownership arrangement.

## Before approving production launch

1. Confirm company domain, legal entity, public contacts and privacy receiver for inbound enquiries.
2. Confirm active/developing/planned status of each division; verify every proposed service and THE 97 World relationship wording.
3. Upload approved original photographs, logo variants, founder portrait and biography; replace illustrative imagery.
4. Review page-by-page copy, prices and claims with the owner; complete accessibility, mobile, performance and form testing.
5. Rebuild public files and root Pages mirror, inspect the draft PR and sign off before merging to main or changing DNS.

## Creative direction

Theme: Contemporary East African enterprise — authentic industrial editorial photography with human-scale confidence. Warm ivory, deep soil and umber, subtle olive and restrained gold. Use clean lines, real material texture, convincing regional environmental details and natural sunrise/late afternoon light. Avoid overprocessing, plastic skin, oversaturated golden glow, implausible vehicle wheels, invented signage, fake corporate uniforms, false country-border maps, company-branded fleets or generic futuristic tech visuals.

Visual treatment: wide cinematic heroes, grounded 3:2 editorial crop, portrait 2:3 mobile hero, 4:5 founder portraits, controlled negative space for HTML headlines. Assets should reinforce a specific service or real approved claim, never invent capacity.

Base brief to append to each non-person image-generation prompt:

"Premium, highly believable editorial photograph in a contemporary East African commercial environment. Natural human scale, tactile surfaces, correct engineering and vehicle geometry, subtle warm earth/ivory/olive palette, soft documentary sunlight, restrained cinematic depth, professional architecture publication quality, clear negative space for headline, no logos, no readable signs, no registration numbers, no text overlays, no watermarks, no badges, no fabricated staff uniforms, no false business-owned facilities, no inappropriate dramatic effects, natural skin and materials, realistic perspective."

## Scene-specific image prompts

Construction: "Well-ordered timber stacks, steel sections, roof sheets and hardware at a grounded East African materials supplier's yard, believable structure, tactile materials, late morning light, empty area at upper left for headline."
Vehicle sourcing: "A single modern four-wheel-drive SUV in a normal paved car inspection setting with realistic reflections, tyres, mirrors and door proportions, restrained premium reportage aesthetic, no dealership trademarks."
Food trade: "Authentic fresh produce and dry staples on pallets, crates of bananas, tomatoes and onions, workers only as distant anonymous context, an ordinary East African distribution space, natural warm side light."
Fitness: "Well-equipped clean functional gym studio with daylight, sensible spacing, no fabricated branded signage, no medical appliances."
Property: "Contemporary attainable residential architecture with carefully scaled interiors, realistic daylight and building materials; label as a representative visual rather than a real listing."
Hospitality: "Welcoming unbranded guest area with timber, stone, greenery and gentle dusk light; no false room-booking promise."
Freight: "One loaded commercial truck parked at a functional warehouse bay, credible pallet handling, practical safety equipment, coherent road and loading geometry."
Digital studio: "Minimal elegant creative technology workspace with laptop, second screen, design samples and hands reviewing a project, screen graphics non-legible placeholders, professional but believable East African office."
Brand design: "Close-up of printed proofing sheets, blank brand mockups, colour studies and geometric grids in tactile real paper materials."
Business automation: "Neutral office screen/workstation for an operations dashboard, no fabricated revenue figures, verification symbols or analytics achievements."

Always append the base brief. Generate image-only material; typeset actual logos, copy, web UI and disclaimers separately.

## Founder page media direction

Hero: front-facing or natural three-quarter approved founder portrait, good daylight, true appearance, crisp eyes and realistic skin, roomy typography space; choose an existing authentic file first.
Story panel: authentic founder in normal business context if available; an illustrative scene must be clearly labelled.
Operations sequence: authentic inspections, paper review, partner conversations and route planning if documented; do not fabricate proof of real deals.
Leadership panel: true verified leadership only; do not invent a full executive team or duplicate founder portraits to imply several staff.
Footer invitation: warm single portrait and company-centred contact CTA.

Founder copy slots: approved name, registered/operational role, 120-word biography, 1–2 sentences of genuine first-person message, vision, leadership responsibilities, portrait permission. No fabricated awards, operating history, geography or quotations.

## Production and export

Source masters: preserve originals and provenance before crop. Desktop hero at least ~2400×1350 when clean source permits; standard editorial around 1800–2400px wide, 3:2 or 4:3; mobile portrait 1200×1800; OG 1200×630; founder portrait as high-quality original with non-destructive crops.

Export responsive AVIF and WebP widths at 480, 768, 1080, 1536 where appropriate, adapting image generation/resizing script and content image keys. Only preload the first viewport's main image. Below-fold media lazy-load. Avoid baked-in headlines or low-contrast text in photography. Test subject focal points on 320px mobile and 1440px desktop.

Manifest metadata for each asset: ID, name, page, source, rights, subject identity consent, whether illustrative, original size, responsive crops, alt text, focal point, generated date if applicable, licensing owner and public approval.

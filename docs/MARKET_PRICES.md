## Quote the load, then the route.

Uganda and South Sudan movements need a cargo-specific quote. A published rate without load capacity, direction, inclusions and date can be misleading. Send the details below for a cost that can be compared.

- **Local and upcountry movements**. Provide pickup and delivery pins, package count, weight, dimensions, ready date and access restrictions. Identify who loads, secures, unloads and signs for the goods.
- **Kampala–Juba**. Quote in an agreed currency against the actual load and carrier. Identify border documentation, waiting time, onward delivery, insurance and receiving arrangements separately. No fixed per-tonne rate is published here.
- **Mombasa–Uganda**. Distinguish port release, clearing, inland carriage and final delivery. A rail tariff is not a road quote and a container rate is not a loose-cargo rate.
- **One cost breakdown**. Ask for carriage, loading, unloading, documentation, insurance, waiting time, border charges and any taxes as separate inclusions or exclusions. The quote should state its validity and timing assumptions.
- **Delivery evidence**. Agree a receiving contact, communication checkpoints, damage-reporting process and proof of delivery before dispatch. Transit timing is confirmed against the route and shipment conditions.

## Timber loads need their own plan.

Timber coordination starts with a measured cutting list and the documents needed for the consignment. A timber load is quoted for its actual dimensions, route and handling requirements.

- **Specify the load**. Send species or acceptable alternatives, piece dimensions, lengths, count, treatment and moisture requirements. Separate boards, poles and structural timber.
- **Choose a measurable basis**. State whether pricing is per piece, cubic metre or a defined load. A price per truck without capacity, loading pattern and dimensions is not comparable.
- **Check the documents**. Identify the supplier, origin, destination and parties responsible for the documents required for that movement. Confirm requirements with the relevant authorities or qualified agent before dispatch.
- **Protect the handover**. Agree loading, restraint, weather protection, unloading access, piece counts and condition checks at both ends. Request timber and transport costs separately.
